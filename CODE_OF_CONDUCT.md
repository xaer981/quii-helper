# Code Of Conduct

This project is intended to be a useful, technical space for people working
with compatible cameras and intercoms.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep issue reports actionable and technical.
- Redact credentials, tokens, private keys, device ids, IP addresses, and logs
  before sharing them publicly.
- Respect device ownership and local laws. Do not use this project to access
  devices you do not own or administer.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, private keys, account data, or raw vendor assets.
- Requests or instructions for unauthorized access to third-party devices.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate these
rules. Repeat or severe violations may lead to blocking from the project.
